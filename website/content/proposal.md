## In one paragraph

I teach at ROC Nijmegen (MBO, Dutch secondary vocational education) and I'm finishing a Master's in Applied AI. I want to do a PhD on **AI-supported citizen neuroscience in vocational education**: building and validating open-source sleep- and dream-monitoring tools together with my own students as co-researchers, extending [Dreamento](https://github.com/dreamento/dreamento) and the Donders Sleep & Memory Lab's open-source line of work. [OpenLucid](/) is the proof that I can do the technical half of this myself.

## Funded through

The [NWO Doctoral Grant for Teachers](https://www.nwo.nl/en/researchprogrammes/doctoral-grant-for-teachers): a scheme that lets a teacher with a permanent appointment do a funded PhD alongside teaching, with up to 0.4 fte released from teaching duties over five years. Deadline for this round: **20 January 2026**.

## Where it stands

Dr. Martin Dresler, at the Donders Institute, has been positive about the research idea itself. But his lab is pure neuroscience, and nobody there has an educational-science background — a real gap for a project whose other half is squarely about teaching and learning. I'm looking for someone to cover that half, as co-promotor or daily supervisor alongside him. NWO places no formal requirements on that role; only the main promotor needs a professorship at an eligible university.

## What I bring

- **[OpenLucid](/)** — an open-source home sleep-EEG system I'm building myself, in public, [day by day](/journey/), mistakes included.
- **[Projectenplaats](https://projectenplaats.nl/lessons)** — a platform I built for ROC Nijmegen that connects students, teachers and companies around real projects, with built-in qualification tracking and AI-supported lessons.
- **[CodeFlow](https://github.com/davorradic/codeflow.live)** — the tool and study behind my Master's thesis: a competency framework for human-AI collaboration in vocational software-development education, tested in a two-day AI-integrated hackathon with MBO students.
