## Everything here stays free

OpenLucid — the app, the server, the videos, the games, the research notes — is free, open source, and needs no account to use. That is not changing, with or without this page.

## What things actually cost

Real numbers, not estimates pulled from nowhere. Episode 1, ["The Bell"](/videos/are-you-dreaming-01-the-bell/) (~1.5 minutes: 14 Higgsfield shots plus an intro card, ElevenLabs narration in a cloned voice, assembled with ffmpeg) cost about **€50** in generation credits — see [how it was made](/journey/2026-11-05-day-06-producing-the-bell/). Everything below scales from that one real data point, so treat the ranges as honest estimates, not quotes.

| What a donation funds | What it actually takes | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| A short episode, Episode 1's length (~1.5 min) | Higgsfield shot generation + ElevenLabs narration — the real cost of the episode already published | **€50** (actual) |
| A full-length episode, the series' real target (6–10 min) | The same pipeline, 4–7× the shots and narration of Episode 1 | **≈€200–350**, depending on length |
| A research-documentary episode (see below) | Everything a full-length episode needs, plus the research time to find, read and fact-check a real published paper before writing the script | **≈€250–400** + research hours |
| A new Neuroscience Games piece | Mostly build time, not generation credits — a new [Brain Atlas](/games/brain-atlas/) topic or a new mini-game is hours of development, the same way the EEG/EOG/EMG trace challenge was built | Time, funded indirectly by general support |
| General, unrestricted | Whatever's next on [the roadmap](/) | Any amount |

## The Martin Dresler documentary, specifically

This is the one people have asked about most, so here's exactly how it would actually get made — not just "we'll figure it out":

1. **Start from a real paper, not a premise.** Pick one of Martin Dresler's actual published fMRI/EEG lucid-dreaming studies at the Donders Institute, and read it properly before writing a single shot — the same discipline [the research notes](/research/) already follow for the rest of this site.
2. **A truth ledger, written first.** Exactly like [Episode 1's](/videos/are-you-dreaming-01-the-bell/) — what's documented in the paper, what's reported by secondary sources, and what's dramatized for the screen, kept clearly separate, with citations.
3. **Ask first.** Martin Dresler is a real, living, named person. This project's own rule for the series — real people drawn stylized, never as likenesses, no invented dialogue presented as history — means he gets to read the script and correct it before anything is published, the same commitment already made for the (also real-person-based) Episode 4 script.
4. **The same production pipeline as Episode 1:** Higgsfield shots with consistent character/setting elements (a scanner, an EEG cap, the lab itself rather than a monastery this time), narration recorded in the cloned voice, assembled with the Python/ffmpeg pipeline — no new tooling, just more of it, because explaining real methodology needs more shots than folklore does.
5. **Published with sources.** Same as every other page on this site: what's real, linked back to the actual paper.

The extra cost over a normal full-length episode isn't the video generation — it's the research and the "ask first" step, both of which take real hours regardless of credits.

## And the Neuroscience Games pieces

These don't run up a generation-credit bill the way episodes do — [Brain Atlas](/games/brain-atlas/) and anything that follows it are built mostly by hand, with Claude Code, the same way the rest of OpenLucid's software is. What a donation buys there is simpler: more hours in the week that go toward the next one instead of toward the day job.
