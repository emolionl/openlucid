## Everything here stays free

OpenLucid — the app, the server, the videos, the games, the research notes — is free, open source, and needs no account to use. That is not changing, with or without this page.

## But it isn't free to build

Every episode, every game, every feature costs two real things: AI-tool tokens, and hours. A donation doesn't unlock anything that wasn't already going to be free. It just means more of both, sooner.

## What a donation actually becomes

Not a vague "support the project" ask — concretely, a donation is roughly the cost of one of these:

- **A research-to-film episode.** Turning a real, published study — for example one of Martin Dresler's fMRI/EEG lucid-dreaming papers at the Donders Institute — into a short watercolor episode like [Episode 1, "The Bell"](/videos/are-you-dreaming-01-the-bell/), truth ledger included, showing exactly what's documented versus dramatized.
- **A new Neuroscience Games piece.** A new explainer inside [Brain Atlas](/games/brain-atlas/), or a new mini-game built around one specific concept — a sleep spindle, an artifact versus a real signal, a sleep-stage transition — the same way the EEG/EOG/EMG trace challenge was built.
- **General runway.** Keeping [the roadmap](/) moving at its current pace, across all four tracks, rather than slowing down when credits run out mid-episode (as happened once already — see [the journey](/journey/)).

## If you'd rather not

That's entirely fine. Reading [the journey](/journey/), starring the repo on [GitHub](https://github.com/emolionl/openlucid), or just using the app is already plenty. This page exists because a few people asked whether they could help, not because anything here is gated.
