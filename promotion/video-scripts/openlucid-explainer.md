**Format:** animated, about 90 seconds, one narrator, bright and friendly cartoon style.
**Audience:** curious people who have never heard of EEG.
**Goal:** after watching, people know what OpenLucid does, that it's free, and that it's honest about what it can't promise.

---

**[0:00] A dark bedroom. A person asleep. A tiny clock ticks to 05:12.**

> **NARRATOR:** Every night you spend about two hours dreaming. And almost never do you notice.

**[0:08] Inside the dream: the sleeper is in a supermarket where the shelves keep rearranging themselves. They shrug and keep shopping.**

> **NARRATOR:** Shelves that move? Totally normal. Says your dreaming brain.

**[0:15] Freeze frame. Title: OpenLucid.**

> **NARRATOR:** OpenLucid is a free, open-source app that tries to help you notice. Meet the night crew.

**[0:20] Moose: a soft headband with big eyes, wobbling on the sleeper's forehead.**

> **NARRATOR:** Moose, your EEG headband. It listens to the tiny electrical whisper of your brain. Slightly dramatic. Slips off at 3 a.m.

**[0:28] The Night Nurse: a clipboard with reading glasses, flipping through thousands of pages.**

> **NARRATOR:** The Night Nurse has read thousands of nights of sleep. Every 30 seconds it guesses: awake, deep sleep, or dreaming. Often right. Always confident. Not the same thing.

**[0:38] The Diary Keeper: an owl with a pocket watch, scribbling in a huge book.**

> **NARRATOR:** The Diary Keeper writes everything down, with the exact time. On your own laptop. Your brain data never leaves your house.

**[0:46] The Night Nurse raises a little flag: "REM!" A speaker plays a soft three-note chime.**

> **NARRATOR:** When the crew is fairly sure you're dreaming, it plays a soft sound. The same sound you practiced with that evening, while asking yourself: am I dreaming?

**[0:56] In the dream: the chime echoes through the supermarket. The sleeper stops. Looks at their hands. Grins.**

> **NARRATOR:** Sometimes, the question follows you into the dream.

**[1:02] The sleeper, inside the dream, looks left-right-left-right. In the bedroom, the Diary Keeper's pen jumps: "05:14 — signal!"**

> **NARRATOR:** And from inside, you can signal back with your eyes. Left, right, left, right.

**[1:10] Morning. The sleeper reads a simple report over breakfast, with one small star on it.**

> **NARRATOR:** Does it always work? No. Sound cues have helped some people in sleep labs; at home it's still an open question. OpenLucid is how you find out, honestly, for yourself.

**[1:20] GitHub page, a laptop, a phone. Text: "Free. Open source. Build it yourself."**

> **NARRATOR:** It's free, it's open, and you can build it yourself, step by step. No programming experience needed.

**[1:27] End card: openlucid.nl · "Educational, not medical advice."**

> **NARRATOR:** So… are you dreaming?
