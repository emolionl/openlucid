# Promotion

Everything we use to tell people about OpenLucid: video scripts, the animated series, and later storyboards, images and posts. It lives in the repository on purpose, so anyone can see how the story is told and help make it better.

```
promotion/
└── video-scripts/     scripts for explainers, devlogs and the "Are You Dreaming?" series
```

The video scripts also appear on the website under **Videos**.

## Rules for everything in here

1. **Educational, not medical.** No promises about health, sleep disorders or treatment. Lucid dreaming is not a therapy, and a consumer headband is not a medical device.
2. **Honest about evidence.** Say what research has shown, how strong it is, and what is still uncertain.
3. **Truth ledger for real history.** Any story about real people ends with a table marking each element as *documented*, *reported* or *dramatized*, with sources.
4. **Real people are asked first.** Before an episode about a living person is published, we contact them.
5. **Stylized, not likenesses.** Animated characters based on real people are not made to look like them.
6. **Humor is welcome.** Kind humor that carries the explanation, never at someone's expense.
